Add events to this calendar

Anyone with permission can add events to a shared Google Calendar called NorCal Vocal Improv + Community Singing Events. To get permission, email norcal-vocal-improv-and-singing@googlegroups.com and ask to be added.

Once you have access:

  1. Open your own calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, etc.) and add your event to the NorCal Vocal Improv + Community Singing Events calendar.
  2. Check that it shows up on the shared calendar.
  3. This page will pick it up within 30 minutes.
