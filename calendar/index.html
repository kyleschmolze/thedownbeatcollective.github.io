This calendar is for community singing events in the Bay Area, where the people attending are the ones singing, such as vocal improv jams, sing-alongs, and drop-in community choirs. It's not for concerts, open mics, or dance events, where attendees mostly watch or listen.

Anyone with permission can add events to a shared Google Calendar called Downbeat Community Singing Calendar. To get permission, email organizers@thedownbeatcollective.com and ask to be added.

Once you have access:

  1. Open your own calendar app (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, etc.) and add your event to the Downbeat Community Singing Calendar calendar.
  2. Check that it shows up on the shared calendar.
  3. This page will pick it up within 30 minutes.
